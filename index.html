01 Services

Every system. Handled with care.

From a routine service to a full engine job — motorcycles and scooters get the same methodical attention, start to finish.

01Complete Bike Servicing
  1. 01

    Complete Bike Servicing

    A thorough periodic service — cleaning, adjustment, lubrication and checks across the whole machine.

  2. 02

    Scooter Repair

    CVT, belt, rollers, starting and body-panel issues on gearless scooters, diagnosed and repaired properly.

  3. 03

    Engine Repair & Maintenance

    Tuning, carburettor and fuel-system work, tappet setting and deeper engine repairs when they are needed.

  4. 04

    Brake & Clutch Service

    Pads, shoes, discs, cables and clutch plates inspected, adjusted or replaced for confident control.

  5. 05

    Oil Change

    Fresh engine oil of the right grade, with filter and drain-plug checks to keep the engine running smooth.

  6. 06

    Battery & Electrical Work

    Battery, wiring, lights, horn, self-start and charging-system faults traced and fixed at the source.

  7. 07

    General Inspection

    A careful check-up of tyres, chain, suspension, brakes and fluids — before a long ride or whenever something feels off.

  8. 08

    Other Mechanical Repairs

    Chain and sprocket, suspension, wheel and general mechanical work. Not sure what it is? Bring it in.

02 Why Diamond

Care isn’t an extra. It’s the process.

We provide professional repair and servicing for all types of two-wheelers. Here is what that means when your vehicle comes in.

  • Careful Vehicle Inspection

    We look the vehicle over before work begins, so the real cause gets fixed — not just the symptom.

  • Professional Repair Approach

    Methodical diagnosis, the right tools and correct procedures, whether the job is big or small.

  • Attention to Every Detail

    Loose bolts, worn cables, a dim bulb — the small things get noticed and treated as important.

  • Multiple Two-Wheeler Brands

    Hero, Honda, Bajaj and TVS — motorcycles and scooters, under one roof.

  • Customer-Focused Service

    We explain what your vehicle needs in plain language and keep you in the loop.
